### 1. Vietnamese Pho

![Vietnamese Pho](/_images/1/199dfa69066aaa1af1f3203209eeca82-medium.webp)

Pho is a noodle soup with beef broth. It is commonly eaten in the morning across Vietnam. Rice noodles and fresh herbs are added at the table. Street vendors serve it hot from early hours.

### 2. Japanese Miso Soup

![Japanese Miso Soup](/_images/d/d49cd4585077aa0e6e5fb22254b3863a-medium.webp)

Miso soup starts the day in many Japanese homes. It contains fermented soybean paste and dashi stock. Tofu or seaweed are frequent additions. It is paired with rice and pickles.

### 3. Korean Seolleongtang

![Korean Seolleongtang](/_images/7/761fb43b3a7079a2a1d362f42c6de03a-medium.webp)

Seolleongtang is a milky beef bone soup. Koreans eat it for breakfast especially in winter. Long-simmered bones create the thick broth. Salt and scallions are added by the diner.

### 4. Chinese Congee

![Chinese Congee](/_images/8/843f9245ea4c906b059a05fdd9d301dc-medium.webp)

Congee is a thin rice porridge served hot. It functions as a breakfast staple in southern China. Ginger and green onions flavor the plain base. Century egg or pork can be stirred in.

### 5. Polish Zurek

![Polish Zurek](/_images/7/7a6afb268dd8cd9a920f9600bbaba3bc-medium.webp)

Zurek is a sour rye soup eaten at breakfast. Fermented rye gives it a tangy taste. Hard-boiled eggs and sausage slices top each bowl. It appears on Polish tables on weekends.

### 6. Nigerian Pepper Soup

![Nigerian Pepper Soup](/_images/e/e76b2ca8cdfb953860f9e1835e9c6936-medium.webp)

Pepper soup is a spicy broth popular at breakfast. Goat meat or fish provides the protein. Scotch bonnet peppers and uziza leaves add heat. It is sold at roadside stalls in Nigeria.

### 7. Turkish Mercimek Corbasi

![Turkish Mercimek Corbasi](/_images/b/be85edec6b36d95bb2b3b8758a474031-medium.webp)

Mercimek corbasi is red lentil soup served warm. Turks often start the day with this simple dish. Lemon wedges and chili flakes are added at the table. It is quick to prepare from pantry staples.
