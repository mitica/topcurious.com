### 1. No Red Clothes Allowed

![No Red Clothes Allowed](/_images/9/927968b5888546b90c900259917bbe6d-medium.webp)

Residents must avoid red garments during harvest season. Elders believe the color attracts bad weather spirits. Young people often sneak in small red accessories anyway. This creates quiet tension between generations.

### 2. Dogs Cannot Bark After Dark

![Dogs Cannot Bark After Dark](/_images/a/a048b517143a9696dda1b56cd942db0e-medium.webp)

Village law fines owners if dogs bark past 10 pm. The rule aims to protect secret fishing trips on the river. Neighbors report violations through anonymous notes. Many families train dogs with muzzles instead.

### 3. Only Women May Plant First Rice

![Only Women May Plant First Rice](/_images/b/b0fbc03e6e2b613851e26a71bce1348c-medium.webp)

Men are banned from the initial rice planting ritual each year. Locals say female hands bring better soil luck. Men handle later weeding and harvesting tasks only. The custom leads to playful arguments during planting week.

### 4. Ghost Money Burning at Weddings

![Ghost Money Burning at Weddings](/_images/a/a9a13f0eb3f6d3e26d902ca8635b4eef-medium.webp)

Couples must burn fake money before the ceremony starts. Families claim it pays off ancestral jealousy. The practice often clashes with modern wedding planners. Guests sometimes hide the burning from city relatives.

### 5. No Mirrors Facing Beds

![No Mirrors Facing Beds](/_images/c/c962534d8de009b59ed3543787ace46e-medium.webp)

Every household covers bedroom mirrors after sunset. Stories warn that uncovered glass invites spirit visitors. Hotels in the area provide cloth covers on request. Tourists frequently forget and get warned by hosts.

### 6. Shared Toothbrushes for Good Luck

![Shared Toothbrushes for Good Luck](/_images/2/21a4808ff56e12c54db720172740715c-medium.webp)

Newlywed couples share one toothbrush for the first month. Elders say it transfers good fortune between partners. The habit surprises outsiders during homestay visits. Some couples quietly switch to separate brushes.
